Finance & Analytics

Profitability, liquidity, debt, and planning tools built from your live sales and inventory data

Revenue, cost of goods sold and inventory value are calculated automatically from your sales and products. Balance-sheet figures (assets, liabilities, equity, expenses) aren't tracked by the POS yet, so enter them once via Edit Financial Inputs and they'll be reused across every metric below. Figures are saved on this device.
How these are calculated
Gross profit = Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold
Net profit = Gross profit − Operating expenses − Interest + Other income − Other expenses
Gross margin = Gross profit ÷ Revenue
Net margin = Net profit ÷ Revenue
ROI = Net profit ÷ Total investment
ROA = Net profit ÷ Total assets
ROE = Net profit ÷ Total equity
How these are calculated
Current ratio = Current assets ÷ Current liabilities
Quick ratio = (Current assets − Inventory value) ÷ Current liabilities
Debt-to-equity = Total liabilities ÷ Total equity
Interest coverage = EBIT ÷ Interest expense
