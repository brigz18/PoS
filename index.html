Frequently Asked Questions

How do employee accounts work?
When you register your business, you become the Owner. From Settings → Employees, you can create Manager, Cashier, and Inventory Staff accounts, each with a scoped set of permissions.
Where is my data stored?
Your data is stored in a cloud database (MongoDB Atlas), encrypted in transit, and isolated per business.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees.
